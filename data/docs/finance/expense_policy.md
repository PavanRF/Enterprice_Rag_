# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Meals while traveling are reimbursed up to $75 per day. Receipts are required for any expense over $25.

Expenses under $500 need manager approval. Expenses up to $5,000 need director approval, and anything above $5,000 needs CFO sign-off. Submit expense reports within 30 days of the expense date.
