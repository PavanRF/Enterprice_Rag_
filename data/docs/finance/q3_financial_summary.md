# Q3 Financial Summary

Q3 revenue reached $12.4 million, up 8% year over year. Operating expenses were $7.9 million, and net margin for the quarter was 14%. Subscription revenue made up 71% of total revenue.

Cash on hand at quarter end was $21.3 million. The finance team forecasts Q4 revenue between $12.9 million and $13.4 million.
